KNOW THE MINISTER

Advocate O. J. Janeesh is a prominent young Indian politician and lawyer who serves as the Minister for Youth Welfare, Sports and Registration in the Government of Kerala. Representing the Kodungallur constituency in the Thrissur district, he is a dynamic leader of the Indian National Congress (INC) and currently holds office as the State President of the Youth Congress in Kerala. Following the United Democratic Front (UDF) victory in the 2026 state assembly elections, Janeesh was appointed as the youngest minister in the cabinet headed by Chief Minister V. D. Satheesan. His rise to political power is widely celebrated as an inspiring story of perseverance, having transitioned from absolute poverty, daily wage labor, and working as a night security guard to becoming a state cabinet minister.
