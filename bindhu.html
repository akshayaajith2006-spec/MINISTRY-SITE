KNOW THE MINISTER

Advocate Bindhu Krishna is a prominent Indian politician, social worker, and lawyer who serves as the Minister for Labour, Animal Husbandry, Dairy Development, and Women & Child Development in the Government of Kerala. Representing the Kollam constituency, she is a highly influential senior leader of the Indian National Congress (INC) and one of only two women appointed to the state cabinet. Following the United Democratic Front (UDF) victory in the 2026 assembly elections, she assumed her ministerial portfolio under the government led by Chief Minister V. D. Satheesan. Known for her fierce grassroot activism, legal proficiency, and women empowerment initiatives, she has previously headed major party wings including the Kerala Pradesh Mahila Congress.
