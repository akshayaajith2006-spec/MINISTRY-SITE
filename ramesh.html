KNOW THE MINISTER

Ramesh Chennithala is a distinguished senior leader of the Indian National Congress (INC) and currently serves as the Minister for Home, Vigilance, and Coir Industry in the Government of Kerala. Representing the Haripad constituency in the Alappuzha district, he is a seasoned statesman with decades of legislative experience, securing his sixth term as a Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA). Following the decisive United Democratic Front (UDF) victory, he was appointed to hold the crucial Home portfolio under the cabinet of Chief Minister V. D. Satheesan. Throughout his political journey, Chennithala has established himself as a core pillar of the Congress party and has served in several important leadership positions.
