KNOW THE MINISTER

Advocate Sunny Joseph is a prominent Indian politician, lawyer, and currently the Minister for Electricity, Environment, and Parliamentary Affairs in the Government of Kerala. Representing the Peravoor constituency in the Kannur district, he is a highly influential senior statesman of the Indian National Congress (INC) and currently holds office as the President of the Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC). Following the United Democratic Front (UDF) victory in the 2026 assembly elections, he was inducted into the 20-member state cabinet under Chief Minister V. D. Satheesan. Known for his strong organizational capabilities and legislative acumen, Joseph smoothly manages critical state infrastructure and sustainable energy portfolios while simultaneously steering the state unit of the Congress party.
