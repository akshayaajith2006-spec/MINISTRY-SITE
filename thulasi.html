KNOW THE MINISTER

K. A. Thulasi, popularly known as Thulasi Teacher, is an Indian politician, academic, and currently the Minister for Welfare of Scheduled Castes, Scheduled Tribes, and Backward Classes in the Government of Kerala. Representing the Kongad (SC) constituency in the Palakkad district, she is a prominent leader of the Indian National Congress (INC) and one of the core female faces in the state government. Following the United Democratic Front (UDF) victory in the 2026 assembly elections, she was appointed to the state cabinet under Chief Minister V. D. Satheesan. Alongside her legislative duties, she serves as a General Secretary of the Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC)
