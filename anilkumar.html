KNOW THE MINISTER

A. P. Anil Kumar is a veteran Indian politician and senior leader of the Indian National Congress (INC) who currently serves as the Minister for Revenue, Survey, Land Records, and Land Reforms in the Government of Kerala. He represents the Wandoor (SC) constituency in the Malappuram district. A seasoned statesman with extensive legislative experience, he secured his sixth consecutive term as a Member of the Legislative Assembly (MLA) in the 2026 state assembly elections. Following the decisive United Democratic Front (UDF) victory, he was appointed to the state cabinet under Chief Minister V. D. Satheesan. Apart from his ministerial duties, he also serves as a Working President of the Kerala Pradesh Congress Committee (KPCC).
